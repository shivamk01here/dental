    The Procedure

    How it works

    1

    Consultation & 3D Scan

    We take a CBCT 3D X-ray to assess bone density and plan the implant position digitally before any procedure begins. Completely painless.

    2

    Implant Placement

    Under local anaesthesia, the titanium post is placed into the jawbone. The procedure takes around 45–60 minutes. Most patients report feeling only pressure — no pain.

    3

    Healing & Osseointegration

    Over 8–12 weeks the implant fuses with your bone. A temporary crown keeps your smile intact during this period.

    4

    Final Crown Placement

    Your permanent ceramic crown is fitted and adjusted for perfect bite alignment. You leave the clinic with your full, natural-looking smile.

    Questions

    Frequently Asked

    The procedure is done under local anaesthesia, so you won't feel pain during the surgery — only mild pressure. Post-procedure discomfort is typically managed with over-the-counter painkillers for 1–2 days. Most patients return to work the next day.

    The titanium implant itself can last a lifetime if maintained properly. The ceramic crown typically lasts 15–25 years before needing replacement. We back every implant with our lifetime warranty programme.

    Most adults with a missing tooth and adequate jawbone are candidates. Even if you have low bone density, we offer bone grafting procedures to make you eligible. The best way to find out is a free 3D scan consultation.

    The cost depends on the implant brand and the crown material chosen. We offer transparent pricing with no hidden charges — book a consultation for a personalised quote. EMI options are also available.

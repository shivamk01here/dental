Got Questions? We’ve got your answers

What dental treatments do you offer?+

We offer a wide range of dental treatments, including preventive care, teeth cleaning, root canal treatment, braces and aligners, dental implants, cosmetic dentistry, and smile-enhancement procedures.

Is dental treatment painful?+

We use modern techniques and advanced technology to make treatments as comfortable and painless as possible. Our team also takes extra care to ensure you feel relaxed throughout your treatment.

Do I need to book an appointment before visiting?+

We recommend booking an appointment so we can give you dedicated time and minimize waiting. You can call us or book your appointment online.

How often should I visit the dentist?+

Regular dental check-ups are recommended to maintain good oral health and identify potential problems early. Your dentist can suggest a visit schedule based on your individual needs.

Are you open on weekends?+

Yes. Dr. Prerna Dental Hub is open 7 days a week, from 10:00 AM to 8:00 PM, making it convenient to schedule your dental visit.
